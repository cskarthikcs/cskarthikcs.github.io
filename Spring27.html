Grading

Your final grade is computed out of 1000 points. Midterm I is worth 275 points, Midterm II is worth 325 points, and the final exam is worth 400 points.

Your total score is the sum of your adjusted Midterm I, Midterm II, and final exam scores. There are no homework assignments or extra-credit activities for this course.

Exam Score Scaling

To account for differences in exam difficulty, the following scaling policy will be applied separately to Midterm I, Midterm II, and the final exam.

For a given exam, let \(\mu\) and \(\sigma\) be the mean and standard deviation, respectively, of the percentage scores obtained by all students. If your original percentage score is \(x\), then your adjusted percentage score is

\[\boxed{y=\min\left\{100,\;\max\left\{x,\;75+12\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma}\right\}\right\}.}\]

If \(\sigma=0\), original scores will be retained. This policy uses a reference mean of 75 and standard deviation of 12, but the adjusted scores are not guaranteed to have that mean or standard deviation, because your score will never be reduced through scaling and will never exceed 100%.

For example, if the exam mean is 60%, the standard deviation is 15 percentage points, and your original score is 50%, your adjusted score is \(75+12(50-60)/15=67\%\). If the formula instead gives a score below your original score, your original score is retained.

The adjusted percentage on each exam is converted back into points using that exam's maximum (275, 325, or 400). The three resulting point totals are added, with no further scaling.
